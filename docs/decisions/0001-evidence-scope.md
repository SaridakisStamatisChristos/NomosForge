# ADR-0001 — Finite verification is not institutional legitimacy

NomosForge searches finite typed grammars and finite declared domains. No result establishes real-world behavior, ethical legitimacy, all possible institutions or universal theorems. Equivalence groups, rights, units, domain bounds and behavior assumptions are inspectable inputs.

Status classification:
- BOUNDED_VERIFIED: policy checks hold on every state in the declared finite domain.
- INFEASIBLE_IN_GRAMMAR: no policy in the specified finite grammar passes the domain.
- MODEL_INFEASIBLE: all admissible outcomes for a legal modeled state fail the chosen axioms.
- UNKNOWN: lack of sound exhaustive evidence, unsupported case or budget.
- CERTIFICATE_CHECKED: only after an independent semantic checker validates the certificate.
