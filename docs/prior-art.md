# Prior art and novelty boundaries

NomosForge composes established ideas rather than announcing a new mathematical result. Related areas include:
- Syntax-guided synthesis (SyGuS), counterexample-guided inductive synthesis (CEGIS), bounded model checking.
- Automated mechanism design and the formal verification/synthesis of social choice mechanisms.
- Automated impossibility and unsatisfiability-core reasoning in social choice.
- Certified policy synthesis in finite Markov decision processes and formal resource allocation.
- Agent-based socioeconomic simulation (e.g. Salesforce AI Economist) for behavioral modeling, with a very different evidence burden.

Selected pointers from the starter pack:
1. [Syntax-Guided Synthesis (2013)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/syntax-guided-synthesis-2/)
2. [Automated Search for Impossibility Theorems in Social Choice Theory (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.3866)
3. [Formal verification and synthesis of mechanisms for social choice (2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S000437022400208X)
4. [Certified policy synthesis for MDPs (IJCAI 2024)](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2024/1)
5. [Salesforce AI Economist repository](https://github.com/salesforce/ai-economist)

**Novelty decision: UNVERIFIED.** A publication proposal requires a detailed updated literature review, a genuinely distinguished formal problem, benchmark baselines against equivalent solver classes, performance/negative evidence, and independent checks.
